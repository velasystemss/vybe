Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no password — the app works the moment you open it.

What happens to my screenshots?

They are processed to generate that one set of replies and are not stored on our servers.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple ID subscription settings, any time. Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

What does the price depend on?

Your App Store region. The exact amount, the trial if you are eligible, and the billing period are all shown in the app before you buy.
