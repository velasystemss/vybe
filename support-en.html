Frequently asked questions

How does VYBE. work?

Upload a screenshot of a conversation, pick the tone you want, and the AI writes three reply suggestions you can send. Tap a suggestion to copy it.

What happens to the screenshot I upload?

It is processed only to generate the replies for that request and is not stored on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, Settings → “Delete my data” removes the records tied to your device. Two records stay, neither of which holds any content: the abuse-prevention counter, which deletes itself within 30 days; and a single timestamp recording that this device has already used its one free generation, which is kept for as long as the free-trial rule applies, because deleting it would hand the same device another free generation.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. If you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

What is free, and what does Premium add?

Each install gets one free generation, for the lifetime of that install. After you have used it, generating replies requires a VYBE. Premium subscription. All eight tones and Story Opener mode are included — nothing is locked to a particular tone.

What do the plans cost, and is there a trial?

There are two plans: weekly and annual. The annual plan starts with a 3-day free trial; the weekly plan has no trial and is charged immediately. The exact price in your currency, the trial length if you are eligible for one, and the billing period are all shown in the app before you buy.
