Frequently asked questions

How does VYBE. work?

Upload a screenshot of a conversation, pick the tone you want, and the AI writes three reply suggestions you can send. Tap a suggestion to copy it.

What happens to the screenshot I upload?

It is processed only to generate the replies for that request and is not stored on our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, Settings → “Delete my data” removes the records tied to your device. The abuse-prevention usage counter holds no content and deletes itself within 30 days.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. If you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

What does the free tier include, and what does Premium add?

The free tier gives you a limited number of generations each day. VYBE. Premium removes that daily limit. All eight tones and Story Opener mode are free for everyone.
